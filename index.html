Good to Know

What does force-free mean?

Training built on rewarding the behaviour you want, with no pain, fear or intimidation. Your dog learns by choice and enjoys it, which makes the learning stick.

What's in the 8 Week Puppy Plan?
  • 30-minute kick-off call on Zoom to get you prepared and book sessions
  • Online plan in weekly sections, with videos and exercises that fit into 5 minutes a day, yours for life
  • 4 private one-hour sessions at your home or local park
  • Unlimited WhatsApp coaching

£485 in South Manchester. Outside the area, the online version is £420 with the four sessions held online.

Where do sessions happen?

At your home or local park around South Manchester, including Didsbury. Online options are available if you're further away.

How should I prepare for a session?
  • Have small, tasty treats ready, a few different kinds
  • Keep your pup's lead, harness and a favourite toy to hand
  • Avoid a big meal just before, so food still motivates
  • Jot down the things you'd most like help with
What's a residential session?

A bootcamp day, full or half, where your dog trains with Janey. You get detailed notes so you can keep building at home.
